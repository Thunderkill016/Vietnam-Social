# Vietnam Social — Product Foundation Pack

Product name: **Vietnam Social**.

A **Signal** is the domain term for an expiring local activity record within Vietnam Social.

Vietnam Social is a map-first product for one question:

> **What can I join or go to around me in the next few hours?**

Initial pilot: a compact Ho Chi Minh City cluster, proposed as **Go Vap + Phu Nhuan + Tan Binh**.

## Why this pack exists

The project should not begin as a generic “social network on a map.” This pack converts market research into explicit product decisions, a narrow validation plan, engineering constraints, and a first implementation task.
